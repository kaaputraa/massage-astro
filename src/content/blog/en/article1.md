After hours on the board, your shoulders, back, and thighs usually feel sore. A massage can help your body feel looser, and it is easiest when it happens right at your villa.

## Common options

- **Deep tissue**: firmer pressure, suited to muscles that feel tight after exercise.
- **Balinese massage**: light to medium pressure, great for all-over relaxation.
- **Reflexology**: focused on the feet, ideal when your legs are tired from walking all day.

## What to tell your therapist

Mention the sorest areas, the pressure you prefer, and any allergy to massage oil. You can ask for lighter or firmer pressure at any time during the session.

## Tips before and after

Drink enough water, and leave about an hour after a heavy meal before your session. Afterwards, rest for a moment before heading out again.
